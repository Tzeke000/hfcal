## Before pitching anyone — do these first

1. **Ethics/JAG consult** (unit legal or ethics counselor). Rules on off-duty
   creations, government time/equipment, and selling to the government
   decide which paths are open. Get the answer in writing.
2. **Trademark cleanup for any commercial variant** — the EGA and "USMC"
   branding require a Marine Corps Trademark Licensing Office license on
   anything sold. The free personal version is a separate question from a
   product.
3. **License decision** — the current CC BY-NC-ND license forbids government
   modification and commercial use. As the author you can dual-license;
   decide before someone asks.
