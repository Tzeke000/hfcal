## HF Field Antenna — Android app v{{VERSION}}

**iPhone and web-app users: nothing to do here — ignore this page.** This is only for the Android app installed from an APK file.

**New install:** download **hf-field-antenna.apk** below on your Android phone → open it → **Install** (allow "install from this source" if asked).

### Already have an older Android APK? One time only
Older APKs were signed with a throwaway key, so Android will say *"App not installed"* until the old one is removed:
1. In the old app: **TOOLS → Saved Shots → EXPORT** any shots you want to keep. Uninstalling deletes saved shots and the field truth log.
2. Uninstall the old app (long-press its icon → Uninstall).
3. Install **hf-field-antenna.apk** from this page.

After this one switch, every update installs straight over the top and keeps your data. The app tells you when a new version is out.
